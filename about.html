About

We are the MAYAK art group. In Russian, mayak means lighthouse, and every project is a new light we raise together.

Our core is based in Amsterdam, with members spread across the world. There is no fixed list of who we are: anyone who helps bring a project to life is part of the crew.

Whatever form a project takes, it starts with an idea worth reflecting on.
