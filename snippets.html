Not 16:9? Add a ratio: <div class="embed" style="--ratio: 4 / 3">

Short video loop (self-hosted, silent)

<figure class="block-loop">
  <video src="../media/FILE.mp4" autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="metadata"></video>
</figure>

Keep loops short and small (under ~10 MB). Longer videos → Vimeo/YouTube.
